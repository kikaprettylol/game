Game Description

This is a two-player competitive riddle game built on strategy, logic, and psychological insight.

At the start of the game, each player secretly sets a hidden answer. Players then take turns trying to uncover their opponent’s secret using one of two actions:

Make a Direct Guess — Say the exact answer.
If you are correct, you win immediately.
If you are wrong, you lose the game.

Ask a Yes-or-No Question — Gather information without directly stating the answer.
The system will respond only with YES or NO.

The first player to correctly identify the opponent’s secret wins.

To ensure fairness and eliminate cheating, all confirmations and final answer validations are handled by an AI judge. The AI determines whether questions are valid, whether responses are accurate, and whether a direct guess matches the hidden answer.

Every match is a battle of deduction, restraint, and timing.

One bold guess can win everything — or end the game instantly.

Think carefully. Ask wisely. Guess with precision.
