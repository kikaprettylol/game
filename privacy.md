# Privacy Policy

Last Updated: Feb 28 2026

Kika (“Company”, “we”, “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our game application (the “Game”).

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect:

- Account information (username, email address)
- Gameplay data (questions, guesses, match results)
- Device information (device type, OS version)
- Log and technical data (IP address, timestamps)
- AI interaction data necessary for game adjudication

We do NOT collect sensitive personal information unless explicitly provided by the user.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use collected data to:

- Operate and maintain the Game
- Process AI-based adjudication
- Improve gameplay and performance
- Prevent cheating and abuse
- Ensure security and system integrity

## 3. AI Processing

Questions and guesses submitted during gameplay may be processed by third-party AI service providers solely for determining Yes/No responses and validating answers.

We do not use gameplay content for unrelated profiling or advertising purposes.

## 4. Data Storage

Game secrets are securely stored using encryption methods. Access is restricted to authorized systems only.

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data.

We may share limited data with:
- AI service providers (for gameplay adjudication)
- Hosting providers (for infrastructure)
- Legal authorities if required by law

## 6. Data Retention

We retain gameplay data only as long as necessary for service operation and dispute resolution.

## 7. Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect data.

## 8. Children’s Privacy

The Game is not intended for children under 13 (or applicable local age requirement).
